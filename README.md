I build **production AI systems** — LangGraph agents, RAG pipelines, multi-step LLM workflows — and I care as much about proving an answer is right as about getting one.

- 🎓 **MSc Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**, University of Birmingham (Russell Group) — **awarded with Distinction**
- 🔬 **Research on evidence retrieval for RAG and large-scale summarisation**, supervised by Dr Mubashir Ali with research guidance from Dr Tuba Gokhan (MBZUAI) — **two papers in preparation**
- 🎤 **Invited research talk** at Aston University, November 2026
- 🚀 **6 AI applications shipped in 12 months**, alongside the MSc — two of them built at competitions I won
- 💼 **3 years of production engineering** (Python / Java / SQL) at Kalki Communication Technologies, serving enterprise energy and utilities clients
- 🏆 **1st Place** Frontier Student Build X Conference · **2nd Place** Google AI Playground London
